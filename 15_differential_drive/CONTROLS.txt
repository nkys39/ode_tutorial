====================================
  15: Differential Drive Robot
  Keyboard Controls
====================================

Movement:
  W  - Move forward
  S  - Move backward
  A  - Turn left
  D  - Turn right
  X  - Stop

View:
  R  - Reset robot position
  Q  - Quit

Tips:
- The robot uses differential drive (independent left/right wheel control)
- Drive wheels (cylinders) provide traction with high friction
- Caster wheels (spheres) provide stability with low friction
- Use W/S for straight movement, A/D for turning
