====================================
  18: RGB-D Camera Sensor
  Keyboard Controls
====================================

RGB Resolution Control:
  1  - VGA resolution (640×480)
  2  - HD resolution (1280×720)
  3  - Full HD resolution (1920×1080)
  H  - Show help

View:
  Q  - Quit

Display:
- Large RGB view (left, upper): High-resolution color camera
- Small depth view (right, upper): Low-resolution depth sensor
- 3D scene (main): Overview with camera frustum visualization

Features:
- Real-time resolution switching
- OpenGL FBO rendering for efficient RGB capture
- Raycast-based depth sensing
- Dual display showing both RGB and depth data

Technical Details:
- RGB: GPU-accelerated, high-resolution (up to 1920×1080)
- Depth: Raycast-based, fixed 64×48 resolution
- Performance: High-res RGB has minimal overhead vs. low-res
