====================================
  19: Robot with LiDAR
  Keyboard Controls
====================================

Movement (with automatic obstacle avoidance):
  W  - Move forward (auto-avoids obstacles)
  S  - Move backward
  A  - Turn left
  D  - Turn right
  X  - Stop

View:
  R  - Reset robot position
  Q  - Quit

Features:
- LiDAR sensor scans 360 degrees around robot
- Automatic obstacle avoidance when moving forward
- Green lines show LiDAR beams
- Red boxes are obstacles detected by LiDAR

Tips:
- Press W to move forward - robot will avoid obstacles automatically
- Robot slows down and turns when obstacles are detected ahead
- Use A/D to manually navigate around obstacles
