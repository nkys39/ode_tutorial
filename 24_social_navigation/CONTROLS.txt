====================================
  24: Social Navigation
  Keyboard Controls
====================================

Mode Control:
  M  - Toggle between Manual and Auto navigation mode

Manual Mode Controls:
  W  - Move forward
  S  - Move backward
  A  - Turn left
  D  - Turn right
  X  - Stop

View:
  R  - Reset simulation
  Q  - Quit

Features:
- Social Force Model for pedestrian simulation
- Socially-aware robot navigation
- Personal space and comfort distance modeling
- Dynamic path planning around people

Display:
- Green robot: Autonomous mobile robot
- Red spheres: Pedestrians walking with social forces
- Blue sphere: Navigation goal
- Arrows: Force vectors (social forces)

Tips:
- Press M to enable Auto mode and watch socially-aware navigation
- Robot maintains comfortable distance from pedestrians
- Observe how robot navigates differently than pure obstacle avoidance
- In Manual mode, use WASD to control robot directly
