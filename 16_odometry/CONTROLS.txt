====================================
  16: Odometry
  Keyboard Controls
====================================

Movement:
  W  - Move forward
  S  - Move backward
  A  - Turn left
  D  - Turn right
  X  - Stop

View:
  R  - Reset robot position
  Q  - Quit

Features:
- Odometry estimates robot position from wheel encoders
- Green path shows estimated position (odometry)
- Blue path shows actual position (ground truth)
- Observe how odometry drifts over time

Tips:
- Drive in circles or figure-8 patterns to see odometry drift
- The longer you drive, the more error accumulates
- This demonstrates why robots need sensor fusion (LiDAR, cameras, etc.)
