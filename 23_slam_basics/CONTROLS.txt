====================================
  23: SLAM Basics
  Keyboard Controls
====================================

Movement:
  W  - Move forward
  S  - Move backward
  A  - Turn left
  D  - Turn right
  X  - Stop

View:
  R  - Reset simulation
  Q  - Quit

Features:
- Simultaneous Localization and Mapping (SLAM)
- LiDAR-based environment mapping
- Real-time landmark detection and tracking
- Particle filter for localization

Display:
- Green robot: Estimated pose
- White dots: Detected landmarks (obstacles)
- Robot trail: Shows movement history
- LiDAR beams: Green lines radiating from robot

Tips:
- Drive around to explore and map the environment
- Watch landmarks being detected and added to map
- The map builds up as you explore new areas
- This is a basic SLAM implementation for learning
